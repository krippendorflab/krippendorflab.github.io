We welcome expressions of interest from prospective PhD students, visiting
students and postdoctoral fellowship applicants whose plans align with our
research.

## PhD and visiting students

Prospective PhD students should apply through the
[standard DAMTP admissions process](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/postgrad/phd-admissions-high-energy-physics-general-relativity-and-cosmology-0)
and indicate their interest in working with Sven. A separate
[expression-of-interest form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSce2OGgXf1tJNv2bAxDSB7-PIA-Puztm_mcJIHDtpzxgc_KQw/viewform)
is available for students with a specific connection to our research
directions. The form helps us identify research fit, but it does not replace
the formal University application process.

Please do not send speculative supervision requests by email. Visiting students
are generally considered only when they have their own funding.

## Postdoctoral fellowships

We are open to discussing externally funded postdoctoral fellowships when the
proposed research fits the lab's programme. Relevant schemes include
[Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships),
the [Royal Society University Research Fellowship](https://www.royalsociety.org/grants/university-research/),
the [STFC Ernest Rutherford Fellowship](https://www.ukri.org/apply-for-funding/our-fellowship-opportunities/ernest-rutherford-fellowships/)
and country-specific fellowship programmes.

Host support is not automatic. Many schemes require departmental approval,
resources or an internal selection before the funder's deadline. Prospective
applicants should complete the postdoctoral fellowship expression-of-interest
[form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdovjqfeLHFsg2l1FktA20UDXWy1B9-AFAVSrkD9MescMSaFw/viewform),
ideally at least three months before the external deadline. Earlier contact is
strongly encouraged where an internal selection is required; late requests may
be impossible to support.

The form is the appropriate first point of contact for fellowship-hosting
enquiries. Submission does not constitute an offer of support, endorsement or
selection by the lab, DAMTP or the University.

## Privacy and data handling

These expression-of-interest forms are separate from the University's formal
admissions and recruitment systems. Responses are collected using Google Forms
and stored in a private Google account administered by Sven Krippendorf. They
are used to assess research fit, supervision or hosting feasibility, and
timing. Where necessary for that assessment, relevant information may be
discussed with appropriate University colleagues.

Please do not submit special-category or other sensitive personal data. If no
application, visit or fellowship discussion proceeds, responses will normally
be deleted within 18 months of the last substantive contact. To request access,
correction or deletion of a response, contact
[slk38@cam.ac.uk](mailto:slk38@cam.ac.uk). Formal applications are handled
under the relevant [University of Cambridge privacy
notices](https://www.information-compliance.admin.cam.ac.uk/data-protection).
Google's handling of information submitted through its service is described in
the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
